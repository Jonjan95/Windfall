# Windfall

**Level your professions for less, see what your farming is really worth, and fish with one key.** Windfall is an all-in-one gold and professions addon for **WoW Forever**. It works across every character on your account and lives right inside the game's own profession window.

Prices come from your **Auctionator** scans (recommended). Without Auctionator, Windfall uses vendor prices only.

---

## Levelling guide for every profession

Pick a profession and a goal (**Level to 75 / 150 / 225 / 300**), and Windfall plans the cheapest way there:

- **Step by step:** which recipe to make from which skill, how many crafts, and where to train each rank. It also tells you when your character's level is too low for the next rank.
- **Real skill-up chances** from the game's own data: orange, yellow and green crafts are weighed by how often they actually give a point.
- **Uses what you already own first,** so materials you have already bought are not wasted.
- **The plan stays put.** Your shopping list only counts down as you buy and craft. Press **New plan** whenever you want it worked out again from current prices.
- **Enchanting rods are planned for:** when a new rod is needed, making it is a step of its own.
- **Create** the current step straight from the route.

Covers Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Leatherworking and Tailoring.

**Herbalism, Mining, Skinning and Fishing** get a gathering guide instead: what to gather at each skill, the zones to find it in, and the ranks to train on the way.

## Shopping list at the auction house and vendors

A small Windfall tab appears on the side of the auction house and of vendor windows. Click it to pull out the shopping list for your route:

- **Have / need** for every material, and what is left to buy with its cost.
- **Show** opens the item in the auction house with the amount you still need filled in. You press Buy yourself.
- **Buy** and **Buy all here** at vendors that sell what you need (thread, vials, dyes...).

## Every character's recipes in one place

Switch between all your characters and their professions with one click, from any character:

- Every recipe with its reagents, what you have, and what your other characters hold.
- Its cost and what it sells for after the auction house cut.
- **Make money:** every recipe on your account that sells for more than it costs, best profit first, with who can make it and how many they can make right now.

## Gold per hour while farming

- **Gold per hour** for what you gather or loot, priced with your auction house scans (or the vendor price when that is higher).
- Two views: **Gathering** counts only herbs, ore, skins and fish, so killing mobs on the way does not pull your rate down. **All loot** counts everything, plus coins.
- **Zone history:** each session is saved per zone, so you can see which spot is worth your time.
- **Node sparkles:** the nearest herb, ore vein or fishing pool gets a sparkle.

## Farming mode

The minimap, large and see-through in the middle of your screen, so herb and ore dots show up right around your character instead of in the corner.

- Clicks go through it to the world. Hold **Alt** to point at a dot and see what it is.
- Works with **GatherMate2**'s remembered nodes.
- Adjustable size, plus a terrain slider to fade the map in behind the dots.

## One-button fishing

Switch the fishing key on and one key casts and reels in. Switch it off and the key goes back to normal. It can also:

- put a lure on
- equip your pole and put your weapons back afterwards
- make the bobber splash louder
- auto loot while you fish

## Buff food reminder

For levelling with experience food: an optional note on screen while you have no Well Fed buff, plus a countdown before it runs out. You choose whether it shows in combat, out of combat, or in cities.

## Getting started

- Open any profession window and click the **Windfall** tab on its right side, or type **/wf**.
- Open each character's professions once so Windfall can read their recipes. After that they are available from every character.
- Scan the auction house with Auctionator now and then to keep prices up to date.

**Commands:** `/wf` (open), `/wf level`, `/wf money`, `/wf farm`, `/wf fish`, `/wf hud`, `/wf food`. Key bindings are under *Options > Keybindings > AddOns > Windfall*.

**Windfall never buys anything at the auction house by itself.** It only finds the item and fills in the amount; you press Buy.
