# Windfall

A personal quality-of-life addon for **WoW Forever**, made for me and my friends. It gathers the small helpers I want while playing into one addon, and it keeps growing as I come up with new ideas, so features come and go between versions.

Right now it helps with:

- **Professions:** every character's recipes in one place, a levelling guide for each profession, and a shopping list at the auction house and vendors.
- **Farming and gathering:** gold per hour, a zone history, sparkles on nearby herbs and ore, and a see-through minimap in the middle of the screen.
- **Fishing:** one key to cast and reel in.
- **Reminders:** an optional note when your food buff is missing or about to run out.

Prices come from your **Auctionator** scans when it is installed. Windfall never buys anything at the auction house by itself.

Type **/wf** in game to open it. The changelog lists what is new in each version.
